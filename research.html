Research interests

Explainable AI & deep learning
Attention-based and dual-stream neural architectures that stay interpretable through explainable-AI techniques.
Intrusion detection
Robust, explainable deep-learning approaches to detecting network intrusions.
Medical & health AI
Classifying brain-tumor MRI scans and diagnosing sleep disorders with hybrid deep-learning frameworks.
Microservices & API design
Building enterprise applications as services connected through RESTful APIs and a gateway, and reducing latency between modules.
Performance engineering
Query optimization, indexing, ORM techniques and Redis caching to make large systems faster and lighter on the server.
Computer science education
Teaching programming, data structures and algorithms, and building learning and student-management platforms.
